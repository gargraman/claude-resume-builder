# Roadmap

Where `resume-builder` goes next, benchmarked against how technical hiring actually works in 2026 — read through the lens of a recruiter processing thousands of resumes across levels (junior → principal). This is a prompt-only Claude Code skill today; the roadmap is honest about what is worth building, what stays prompt-side, and what is deliberately out of scope.

Each category lists **what it is**, **why a recruiter cares**, and **concrete capabilities**. Items shipped in the current pass are marked ✅; everything else is open and PR-friendly.

---

## Why now — the strategic tension

The skill currently optimizes hard for a **2019-era ATS model**: maximize keyword density, exact-match tokens, inject each hard keyword at least twice. That still matters — the keyword layer has not gone away.

But 2026 screening is **two-layer**. Behind the keyword ATS sits an **LLM semantic layer** that summarizes each resume, scores it against the role, and drafts recruiter notes. That layer — and the large majority of hiring managers who now rely on it — actively **penalizes** keyword-crammed, uniform, "too perfect," AI-sounding resumes.

The uncomfortable finding: **our current optimization can get a resume flagged as AI-generated by the very layer that now gatekeeps.** Several categories below (A, B, E in particular) exist to resolve this tension — to satisfy the keyword layer *without* tripping the semantic one — not merely to add surface features.

The guiding principle for everything here: **prefer real, user-supplied evidence over inference.** The strongest resume is the true one, told well — not the most heavily optimized one.

## Categories

Ordered by the sequencing recommendation below: front-of-pipeline intake first, then the highest-value 2026 shifts, then breadth and robustness.

### I. Hiring-manager pre-screen & completeness intake *(front of pipeline)*

**What it is.** A new layer inserted as **Phase 1.5** (after resume load, before JD analysis) that reads the base resume through an experienced hiring-manager lens, reports what's missing, and asks the user to supply the real details *before* any tailoring begins.

**Why a recruiter cares.** Recruiters reject on completeness and clarity long before fit: missing contact links, un-quantified bullets, ambiguous dates, buried titles, and vague tech all cost interviews. Fixing these upfront with the candidate's real information is strictly better than inferring or hedging later.

**Capabilities:**
- **Missing-details report**, categorized: contact & links (email/phone/LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio); un-quantified bullets; unclear scope/seniority (team size, ownership, system scale); vague tech ("Cloud" vs "AWS EC2/S3"); timeline integrity (gaps, missing/ambiguous dates, overlaps); buried titles; education/cert completeness for the level.
- **Interactive intake** — asks for the highest-value gaps one at a time, skippable (reuses the Phase 3.5 mechanic); answers merge into the working profile.
- **Anti-fabrication synergy** — fills gaps with real detail from the user instead of Phase 4 LOW-confidence inference.
- Distinct from **Phase 3.5** (resume-vs-JD requirement gaps): this is *intrinsic* resume quality, and runs *before* the JD is analyzed.
- Deterministic parts (missing links, un-quantified-bullet count, date-gap detection) come from a completeness script; the "what reads weak" judgment stays LLM.

### A. Authenticity & AI-detection resistance *(highest-value, timeliest)*

**What it is.** Make the output read as a real human's resume, and scrub the *input* for planted traps.

**Why a recruiter cares.** This is the dominant 2026 shift. AI-fingerprinted resumes get auto-down-ranked or rejected. Hidden-text injection is now common enough that screening vendors report it as a material share of deception cases.

**Capabilities:**
- Expand the forbidden-terms list with 2026-flagged words: *spearheaded, leveraged, pivotal, intricate, showcasing, delve, realm, robust, synergy*.
- **Em-dash frequency guard** — a classic AI tell; cap at roughly human frequency (~1 per 500 words), not one every 50–80.
- **Sentence-cadence variation** — break uniform bullet length/structure that reads as machine-generated.
- **Metric-defensibility check** — flag suspiciously round/perfect numbers; enforce "if you can't walk an interviewer through it, cut it."
- **Prompt-injection / hidden-text scan** — detect white-text or injected instructions in the *base* resume; guarantee our *output* never emits hidden text.
- **Voice preservation** — retain the candidate's authentic phrasing instead of homogenizing it.

### C. Seniority / leveling calibration *(serves the junior → principal range)*

**What it is.** We already *detect* level (`references/job-analysis.md`); this makes us *rewrite to the level's signal*.

**Why a recruiter cares.** A bullet that would impress at mid-level reads as under-leveled for staff. Recruiters down-rank resumes whose evidence doesn't match the target level — in either direction.

**Capabilities:**
- **Level-targeted bullet rewriting**: junior = shipped outputs; senior = judgment, scope, blast-radius, what *not* to build; staff/principal = org-wide influence, adopted processes, "engineers whose week got better."
- **"Is this bullet senior enough?" check** — flag bullets indistinguishable from a lower level.
- **Over/under-leveling detection** — warn when the evidence doesn't support the JD's level.
- **Page-length guidance by level** (1 page under ~10 yrs; 2-page ceiling).

### E. Recruiter-side simulation & batch *(the "I am the recruiter" lens)*

**What it is.** Today we optimize but never *simulate* how a recruiter or the AI layer would actually score and rank the result.

**Why a recruiter cares.** It closes the loop — it tells the candidate what the person on the other side will see and decide, before they submit.

**Capabilities:**
- **Two-layer score simulation**: (1) ATS parse + keyword score, (2) LLM semantic screen → drafted recruiter note + shortlist yes/no + reasons.
- **Reject-reason prediction** — the most likely reason this resume gets filtered.
- **6–8 second scan simulation** — what a human sees in the first glance.
- **Batch mode** — score and compare *many* resumes against one JD (the literal "process thousands" use case). The skill is single-resume today.

### F. Job-search pipeline breadth *(competitor parity)*

**What it is.** Full suites (Jobscan, Teal, Prentus, Wonsulting) span Build → Optimize → Apply → Track → Prep. We cover Build/Optimize plus light prep.

**Why a recruiter cares.** Indirectly — a coherent application (resume + matching cover letter + tuned LinkedIn) signals seriousness and consistency.

**Capabilities:**
- ✅ **Short opt-in cover letter** — shipped this pass (see below). Reuses the company intel we already fetch.
- **LinkedIn** headline/about/skills optimization (a different medium than the ATS resume).
- **Interview-prep expansion** — mock-interview question sets generated from the JD.
- Tracking / autofill — likely out of scope for a stateless skill; noted, not planned.

### B. Semantic / skills-based matching *(evolve past pure keyword)*

**What it is.** Rebalance the matching model from exact-match repetition toward semantic coverage.

**Why a recruiter cares.** The semantic layer rewards genuine capability coverage and punishes stuffing. Skills-based hiring rewards transferable evidence over title-matching.

**Capabilities:**
- Rebalance density rules toward **semantic coverage** over exact-match repetition (keep the keyword layer satisfied without stuffing).
- **Recency & duration weighting** in fit scoring — a skill used 4 years in the current role should outrank a 7-year-old 6-month mention. The current model ignores both.
- Stronger transferable/adjacent capability framing.

### D. Impact & evidence quality *(recruiter scoring rubric)*

**What it is.** Grade every bullet on whether it shows *impact* or just *activity*.

**Why a recruiter cares.** It's the core of how strong resumes are scored: verifiable outcome > qualitative outcome > task description.

**Capabilities:**
- **Activity-vs-impact classifier** per bullet — flag task descriptions with no outcome.
- **Evidence-strength scoring** per bullet (verifiable metric > qualitative > task).

### H. Format conversion & template transforms

**What it is.** Today conversion is coupled to tailoring (Phase 1 loads docx/pdf → text; Phase 6 emits md → docx/pdf). Make it a **first-class, standalone capability** requiring no JD.

**Why a recruiter cares.** Only indirectly — but candidates constantly need "make this ATS-safe" or "convert this" without re-tailoring, and a broken multi-column PDF fails the parse before any scoring happens.

**Capabilities:**
- ✅ **Honest supported-format display** — shipped this pass (the Phase 0 prompt now names readable inputs and produced outputs plainly; see below).
- **Arbitrary format conversion**: docx→md, pdf→md/docx, txt→structured md, md→docx/pdf.
- **Template / layout transforms**: reflow any resume into ATS-safe single-column; switch section ordering between IC and leadership layouts — without re-tailoring content.
- **Round-trip fidelity**: normalize to ATS-safe while preserving content verbatim (ties into A's hidden-text scan).
- **Standalone invocation**: "convert my resume to X" short-circuits the 7-phase flow.

### G. Pipeline robustness / correctness

**What it is.** Housekeeping and coverage breadth.

**Capabilities:**
- ✅ **Version reconcile** — `plugin.json` and `SKILL.md` are both `2.1.0` as of this pass (was 1.0.0 / 2.0.0 drift).
- Broaden adjacency maps beyond core eng ecosystems (security, mobile, ML-platform/MLOps, data-science depth, DevRel, eng-adjacent PM).
- Optional **parse-back test** — regenerate → re-extract to verify the ATS parses the output as intended.

## Shipped in this pass

Four behavior changes landed in `SKILL.md` now; the rest of each category stays open above.

1. **Ruthless voice & review stance** (persona) — the skill now runs with no sugar-coating: blunt, specific critique of the *resume* (not the person), options presented recommended-first, and an explicit "say when something is a bad idea" rule threaded across every printed block (JD analysis, fit assessment, gap prompts, augmentation results, final summary). Sets the *stance* that Categories I/C/D will later back with mechanics.
2. **Multi-source supplementary intake** (Category I / B) — optional LinkedIn URL, GitHub profile/repos, and pasted notes merge into one working profile with source provenance. LinkedIn is best-effort with a paste/PDF fallback (it blocks scraping — no false promises). GitHub reads owned repos' READMEs for real, verifiable tech; forks/stars are not treated as the candidate's own work. User-supplied evidence is preferred over LOW-confidence inference.
3. **Opt-in cover letter** (Category F) — at delivery the skill offers a short (~150-word) cover letter tailored to the company, generated only on "yes," grounded in existing company intel and the two strongest fit signals, held to the same anti-fabrication and forbidden-phrase rules as the resume (short = defensible; long = AI-flag liability).
4. **Honest output-format prompt** (Category H) — the existing Phase 0 format menu now names the readable **inputs** (`.md, .txt, .pdf, .docx`) and produced **outputs** (Markdown, DOCX, PDF), and states plainly that Markdown is always produced as the working copy (it is the conversion source and the report cross-references it). True standalone conversion remains open under H.

**Deferred sub-items:** LinkedIn/interview-set generation (F), true standalone format conversion and template transforms (H), and moving the format prompt from Phase 0 to delivery all remain open.

## Suggested sequencing

Cheapest-high-impact first:

1. **I — hiring-manager intake** — biggest quality lift, mostly reuses existing intake mechanics.
2. **A — authenticity** — directly counters the 2026 auto-reject; largely a lint pass plus rule additions.
3. **C — leveling calibration** — high value across the junior→principal range; prompt-side rewriting.
4. **H — standalone conversion** — self-contained, useful on its own, unblocks round-trip fidelity.
5. **E — scoring & batch** — the recruiter-simulation payoff; needs the score script for stable ranking.
6. **F — breadth** — LinkedIn + interview sets, once the core is solid.
7. **B / D / G** — folded in alongside the above (scoring rebalance, evidence grading, coverage/robustness).

## Tooling & scripts

Ruthless split: build a script **only** where the LLM is unreliable — exact counting, arithmetic, or reading properties invisible in extracted text. Everything judgment-based (leveling rewrite, semantic screen, cover letters) stays prompt-side; a script there adds nothing.

**Dependency-light constraint (public MIT plugin):** POSIX shell + `awk` + `python3` stdlib only; `pandoc` stays *optional* as today. Avoid new heavy deps — e.g. detect docx white text by `unzip`-ing the XML and grepping, **not** by adding `python-docx`. Bundle under `skills/resume-builder/scripts/` and `.../assets/`; `Bash` is already in `allowed-tools`.

| Script / asset | Does | Why a script (not the LLM) | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scripts/completeness-check.sh` | Scans base resume for missing links, un-quantified bullets, ambiguous/missing dates, employment gaps → structured report | Exact, reproducible gaps the LLM would miscount or miss | I |
| `scripts/authenticity-lint.sh` | Counts em-dash density, flagged-term hits, first-person pronouns, unicode-bullet chars, sentence-length variance, date-format inconsistency → pass/warn | LLMs miscount occurrences and rationalize; a regex/awk pass is exact | A, D |
| `scripts/hidden-text-scan.sh` | `unzip` docx → grep XML for white/vanish runs + injected-instruction patterns | White text is stripped/invisible in extracted plain text — the LLM cannot see it | A |
| `scripts/convert.sh` | Standalone any→any conversion wrapping pandoc with the ATS-safe template + 3-tier PDF fallback (xelatex→wkhtmltopdf→libreoffice) | Deterministic invocation + fallback chain; reused by Phase 6 and the standalone path | H |
| `assets/ats-reference.docx` | Pandoc reference doc enforcing single-column, standard headers, safe fonts | An asset, not logic — guarantees ATS-safe styling over pandoc defaults | H |
| `scripts/score.sh` | Weighted overall + band from per-requirement sub-scores; in batch mode ranks N resumes into a table/CSV | Arithmetic + stable ranking across many candidates — LLMs drift on both | E |
| `scripts/parse-back-check.sh` | Generated md → docx/pdf → re-extract → diff hard-keyword coverage | Verifies the *actual* ATS parse; can't be asserted by reasoning about the source | A, G |
| version-sync check | Assert `plugin.json` and `SKILL.md` versions match | Cheap guard against version drift recurring | G |

**Stays prompt-only (no script):** B (semantic reasoning), C (level rewrite), E's semantic screen + recruiter-note draft, F (cover letter / LinkedIn / interview generation). The linter and score scripts *feed* these phases with hard numbers; they don't replace the judgment.

## Sources

The 2026 recruiting reality this roadmap benchmarks against:

- Two-layer screening (keyword ATS + LLM semantic layer) and AI-detection penalties in resume review.
- AI-writing red flags recruiters and detectors flag (buzzword clusters, em-dash frequency, uniform cadence, suspiciously round metrics).
- Hidden-text / prompt-injection deception in submitted resumes (screening-vendor reports).
- Skills-based and recency-weighted matching over exact-title keyword matching.
- Leveling-signal expectations across junior → staff/principal.
- Full job-search suites (Jobscan, Teal, Prentus, Wonsulting) as the competitive baseline for pipeline breadth.
